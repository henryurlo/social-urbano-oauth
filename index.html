social-urbano

An owner-operated personal automation app maintained by henryurlo.

Its currently documented use is backing up selected personal files to the owner's Google Drive with rclone and restic. Files are encrypted on the owner's server before they are uploaded; backups can be checked and restored by the owner.

This is an information page for Google's authorization screen. The app is for the owner's personal use and has no public signup.
