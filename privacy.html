Privacy policy

This policy describes the currently documented personal backup use of social-urbano, an owner-operated Google authorization app maintained by henryurlo.

Google access and purpose

The owner grants Google Drive access so rclone and restic commands running on the owner's server can create, check and restore encrypted backups. The configured Google Drive permission permits access to all Drive files; it is broader than the backup folder. The backup job is configured to use its designated private backup folder.

Data handled and storage

Selected files are encrypted locally before upload to Google Drive. Drive metadata, including file and folder identifiers, names and available storage, is used to operate the backup. Authorization credentials are stored privately on the owner's controlled computer, not on this website. Google stores the encrypted repository in the owner's Drive account.

Sharing and use

This backup job does not sell Google user data, send it to advertisers, or use it to train machine-learning models. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control and retention

The owner controls backup retention and deletion. Access can be revoked in Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized operations but does not delete existing backup files; the owner can delete those separately from Drive. Local backup copies and credentials are managed separately on the owner's computers.

This website

These static pages contain no sign-in form, analytics script or tracking cookie. The hosting provider may process normal web-request information under its own privacy policy.

Questions and changes

Contact the maintainer through GitHub. This policy must be updated before the app's data practices change.

Terms of use

This app is maintained for its owner's personal use. No public service, account registration, subscription or support commitment is offered. These pages do not grant access to the owner's Google account or backups.
